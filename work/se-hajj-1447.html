Hajj 1447 storyboard

Every scene generated with AI.

A film storyboard for Saudi Energy’s Hajj 1447 readiness campaign: one filming day for each stage of Hajj, each with its location, timing, interview and voice-over. Every scene of the engineers among the pilgrims was generated with AI on a Magnific Spaces canvas, so the client could see the film before a camera rolled.
