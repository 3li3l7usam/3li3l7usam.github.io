National Day 96

Our pride is our industry.

A national day campaign built on one visual idea: Saudi industry, seen through the people who drive it. Every scene was directed and generated with AI, then unified into one visual language for posters, greetings, stickers and city screens.

Client
MODON
Role
Concept, visual direction and design
Scope
Posters, greetings, stickers, screens
AI tools
Magnific Spaces, Nano Banana Pro, Photoshop

Pride, told through the people of industry.

The objective
Celebrate National Day as a story about Saudi industry and the people behind it.
The idea
‘Our pride is in our industry’: every scene puts a Saudi figure at the heart of production.
Visual direction
Directed AI scenes in deep industrial blues and warm golden light, with heritage patterns inside modern factories.
