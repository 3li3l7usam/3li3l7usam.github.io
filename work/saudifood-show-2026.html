Saudifood Show 2026

One theme, every touchpoint.

A dedicated theme and the full design suite for MODON’s participation in The Saudifood Show, from outdoor and VIP branding to invitations and digital banners.

Client
MODON
Role
Event theme and design
Scope
Outdoor, VIP, print, digital
AI tools
Magnific Spaces, Photoshop, Illustrator

One theme, from the roadside to the VIP lounge.

The objective
Present MODON as the home for food manufacturers and draw investors to its pavilion.
The idea
One theme split into four value messages, each on its own touchpoint along the visitor’s path.
Visual direction
MODON greens and angular facets framing factory scenes of Saudi women at work.

Step by step

From the first sketch to the final file.

  1. Step 01

    Sketch the site

    Pen and paper, site photos

    A row of flags in front of the pavilion sketched over the real entrance, with the empty site as the base plate.

  2. Step 02

    Design flat

    Illustrator, Photoshop

    Flags, fences and gates designed flat in one green family, with the Arabic and English messages paired on every piece.

  3. Step 03

    Place and render

    Photoshop, Magnific Spaces

    The artwork placed on the site photo, then re-lit in Magnific so light, shadow and fabric read as one photograph.

Behind the scenes

From canvas to venue

  1. ReferenceFactory floors, robotic lines and MODON buildings collected as one board.
  2. GenerateImage Generator nodes branch every scene into options before one is chosen.
  3. ApplyThe chosen scenes framed in the theme’s green facets on flags, fences, gates and the VIP wall.
