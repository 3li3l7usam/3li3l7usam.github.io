Your turn to shape the workplace.

An internal MODON initiative inviting employees to help shape their work environment. One calm visual system guides colleagues from the first teaser through nominations and voting to the winners’ announcement.

Client
MODON
Role
Identity and campaign design
Scope
Teasers, voting, surveys, results

Change starts with your turn.

The objective
Invite employees to shape their workplace through surveys, ideas and voting.
The idea
‘It’s your turn’: from teaser to winners, people see their input change something.
Visual direction
Calm MODON blues with a lime highlight on every call to action, friendly and practical.
