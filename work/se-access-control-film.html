Access control film

A storyboard built with AI scenes.

A storyboard for an introductory film on Saudi Energy’s Lenel access-control system for industrial security, in three chapters, each scene with its shot and voice-over. The offices, gates, control rooms and staff were all generated with AI on a Magnific Spaces canvas.
