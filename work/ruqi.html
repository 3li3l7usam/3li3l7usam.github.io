Ruqi

Every voice, one campaign.

An internal campaign inviting MODON employees to take part in the Participation Enhancement initiative, from nominations and workshop invitations to voting. One unified campaign, conceived, directed and designed end to end.

Client
MODON
Role
Concept, direction and design
Scope
Posters, invitations, voting

A campaign employees can see themselves in.

The objective
Raise employee participation at every stage, from nominations to workshops and voting.
The idea
‘Ideas start with you’: employees shown as the authors of change. Nominate, participate, make an impact.
Visual direction
Real colleagues, deep navy and light-blue type for an authentic tone rather than a promotional one.
