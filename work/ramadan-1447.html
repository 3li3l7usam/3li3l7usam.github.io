Ramadan 1447

A theme for the season.

A seasonal theme for MODON: a hand-lettered wordmark, a blue-to-green light gradient and one layout system, applied to greetings for staff and investors and a download card.

Client
MODON, Ministry of Industry
Role
Theme and design
Scope
Wordmark, greeting posters, cards

One season, one light, every audience.

The objective
One Ramadan identity for every greeting, from the minister to staff and investors.
The idea
Light as the symbol of the month, around a hand-lettered ‘Ramadan Kareem’ wordmark.
Visual direction
A blue-to-green glow taken from MODON’s own palette, so the season feels part of the brand.
