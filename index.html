The real canvases: references, generations and finished designs side by side, the way each campaign was built in Magnific Spaces.

How I work with AI

Five steps and one rule: AI speeds up the making, never the thinking. Every image is directed, chosen and finished by hand.

  1. Idea

    Claude and ChatGPT

    The brief analysed and the idea built, down to the copy direction and the prompts.

  2. Reference

    Magnific Spaces

    Location, light, people and cultural detail gathered as one board.

  3. Generate

    Magnific with Nano Banana Pro

    Scenes directed and iterated side by side on one canvas.

  4. Refine and animate

    Photoshop, Higgsfield, Flow

    Upscaling, retouching and compositing, then key frames turned into motion.

  5. Design

    Adobe Creative Cloud

    Typography, layout and roll-out to every format and screen.
